# Provenance of the correlation constant a_corr

**Status: working draft, not submitted for peer review.**

`acorr_derivation.pdf` (built from `acorr_derivation.tex`) is a provenance record: it traces how the
substrate corpus treats the atomic correlation constant a_corr, step by step, to file, function and
commit, with each premise and its status. Its findings:

- In the corpus a_corr = eps_2 - eta_2 is the high-Z, same-centre, two-electron second-order
  correlation coefficient of the Coulomb comparison model. eta_2 is derived in closed form; eps_2 is a
  published value, so the corpus number is a diagnostic, not a substrate prediction.
- The corpus splits a_corr into two parts in three senses: contact block plus non-contact remainder;
  s-wave (about 31 percent) plus higher partial waves (about 69 percent), of which the coincidence limit
  fixes only the large-l tail; and charge tail plus two-centre correlation. None of the splits
  determines a_corr from the master Lagrangian.
- The frozen two-centre trial energy is closed as an exact differential equation with an exact germ,
  hence computable by series; the true static minimum is fenced but has no series; the identification
  with the moving-mode correlation is a registered, undischarged commitment. The physical a_corr is
  open.
- Routes tried (coincidence match, proximity cap, strain tail and wake, cuspless kernel, order-4
  Picard-Fuchs, M11 true minimizer, harmonium) and why each does not close are listed with sources.

The companion paper `../two_center_born_infeld/` treats the static two-centre problem in full.
